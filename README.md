### Rohit Ravi

Undergraduate researcher at NYU's Center for Data Science (Courant Institute). I build machine-learning models on neural, genetic and clinical data, then try to break them, and I publish both.

**Selected work**

- [**meta-emg**](https://github.com/rohit-ravi2/meta-emg): reproduced Meta's released sEMG handwriting models, then halved their character error rate (30.1% → 15.0%) with per-user fine-tuning and a de-leaked language model.
- [**neural-control**](https://github.com/rohit-ravi2/neural-control): whole-brain *C. elegans* activity; an identity-indexed readout that doubles a published baseline (0.223 vs 0.105).
- [**worm-graph-dynamics**](https://github.com/rohit-ravi2/worm-graph-dynamics): across 38 held-out animals, the connectome predicts neural activity no better than degree-matched shuffled graphs.
- [**clinical-trial-failure-prediction**](https://github.com/rohit-ravi2/clinical-trial-failure-prediction): Phase 3 outcome prediction at AUROC 0.86, with a full label-leakage audit.
- [**asd-myelin-timing**](https://github.com/rohit-ravi2/asd-myelin-timing), [**hypermobility-genetics**](https://github.com/rohit-ravi2/hypermobility-genetics): statistical genetics with LDSC and MAGMA on public GWAS.
